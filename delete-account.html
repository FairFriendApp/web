Delete Your Account
Fair Friend · com.fairfriend.app

You can delete your Fair Friend account and its associated data at any time. This page explains how, and exactly what is removed.

Deleting from within the app

If you still have Fair Friend installed, this is the fastest way and takes effect immediately:

  1. Open the Fair Friend app and sign in
  2. Go to My Account
  3. Tap Manage Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Type your email address to confirm

Deleting your account cannot be undone. You will be signed out immediately and will not be able to sign in again with the same account.

Requesting deletion without the app

If you have already uninstalled the app, or cannot sign in, email us and we will delete your account for you:

Email support@fairfriend.app from the address associated with your account, with the subject Delete my account.

We will confirm your identity, complete the deletion, and reply to confirm when it is done. Requests are normally handled within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login and authentication details
  • Your profile: name, email address, phone number, and location
  • All photos you uploaded, removed permanently from our storage
  • Your contact details on any business listing you created

What is retained, and why

Some records are kept so that event organisers retain an accurate history of their events. These are hidden from the app and are no longer linked to you personally:

  • Business listing records — archived and removed from public view, so customers can no longer see them
  • Payment records for any fees paid, kept for accounting purposes and no longer linked to your account
  • A record of the deletion itself, including the date, so we can demonstrate that your request was carried out

If you would like these records removed as well, say so in your email and we will review the request.

Customers browsing without an account

You do not need an account to browse vendors in Fair Friend. If you have only used the app to search and browse, we hold no account or personal information about you, and there is nothing to delete.

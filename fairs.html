The problem

Your fairgoers can't buy from vendors they can't find.

Big grounds, hundreds of vendors.

The bigger your fair, the more ground there is to cover, and the easier it is for guests to miss what they came for.

Paper maps don't search.

A guest craving fried dough or looking for a vendor they loved last year can't type that into a printed map.

Missed vendors mean missed sales.

Every vendor who goes unfound has a slower fair, and slower fairs make it harder to bring great vendors back.

A better fair experience

Your vendors, right in
every guest's pocket.

Fairgoers find what they're looking for, and vendors get found by guests looking for exactly what they sell. All for free. The result: happier guests and busier vendors who come back year after year.

Menus, photos & prices

"Show Me The Way"

A satellite map pins each vendor's exact spot on your grounds and shows guests where they are, so they can walk right to it.
